The capture shows both absorption zones, BUY / SELL arrows, sweep markers, the Entry / SL / TP1–TP3 ladder and the dashboard.

Rules for any capture added here:

1. **Dark chart background.** Zone transparency is blended toward the chart background colour, so a light theme will look nothing like the original.
2. **One capture, one claim.** Don't caption a screenshot with a win rate or a return. This is an indicator that draws levels; it does not trade them.
3. **Check for label collisions before publishing.** Zone captions sit at the left of the box and target captions at the right of their rays; at similar prices they can overlap (KNOWN_ISSUES #16).
